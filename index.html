Our Heritage

A Taste of Morocco's Finest Traditions

Nestled in the vibrant heart of Casablanca's Hay Mohammadi neighbourhood, Asswab is more than a restaurant — it is a celebration of Moroccan culinary heritage. From the slow-braised tagines perfumed with saffron and preserved lemon, to the hand-rolled couscous prepared every Friday, every dish honours the recipes of our ancestors.

We believe that food is the most honest expression of culture. At Asswab, we serve the real Morocco — generous, warm, and unforgettable.
